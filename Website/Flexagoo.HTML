About Flexagoo

Flexagoo is a people‑powered way to move things from one place to another. We connect folks who need to send something with travelers already heading that way. It’s simple, fast, and built around real people helping real people.

For Senders

Sending something shouldn’t feel like a chore. With Flexagoo, you tell us what you need delivered and where it’s going — and we match you with a traveler already on that route.

For Travelers

You’re already going — why not earn along the way? Flexagoo lets you pick up small deliveries that fit naturally into your route.

How Flexagoo Works

1. Sender Creates a Request

Describe your item, pickup, and drop‑off.

2. Traveler Accepts & Delivers

Verified travelers pick up your item and deliver it along their route.

3. Delivery Completed

Traveler hands the package to the receiver — simple and secure.
